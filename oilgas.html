Oil & Gas Operations & Support

At FOLSME International, we deliver comprehensive support to the oil and gas industry in Nigeria. Our expertise spans exploration, production, transportation, and refining operations.

We prioritize safety, environmental protection, and operational efficiency. Our team provides technical consulting, maintenance services, and compliance audits to ensure seamless and sustainable operations.

Below are our key capabilities in oil and gas support.

Key Capabilities

Exploration support and field surveys

Pipeline construction and maintenance

Installation of processing and pumping stations

Technical consulting and safety audits

  • Nigeria is Africa's largest oil producer, with significant reserves in the Niger Delta.
  • The oil and gas sector accounts for over 90% of Nigeria's export earnings.

  • We maintain a 100% safety compliance record in operations.
  • Our environmental impact assessments ensure sustainable practices.
