About FOLSME International

Pioneering mining, energy, engineering, and trade with excellence since 2015.

Who We Are

FOLSME International Limited, incorporated in 2015, is a Nigerian enterprise committed to excellence in mining, engineering, and trade. With our headquarters in Osogbo, Osun State, and operational presence in Erio-Ekiti, we have built a reputation as a trusted partner delivering sustainable solutions across multiple sectors.

Guided by a vision of growth and innovation, we combine cutting-edge technology with skilled expertise to execute projects of national and international relevance. Over the years, FOLSME International has expanded its footprint into energy, construction, logistics, oil & gas, and commerce, positioning us as a multi-sectoral leader with strong roots in Nigeria's industrial ecosystem.

At our core, we believe in integrity, innovation, and impact. These values shape how we serve our clients, develop our workforce, and contribute positively to the communities in which we operate.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the leading Nigerian enterprise in mining, engineering, and trade by delivering sustainable, innovative, and high-quality solutions across all the sectors we serve.

Our Mission

To provide world-class engineering, construction, and logistics solutions, leveraging technology and skilled professionals, ensuring sustainable growth and unmatched client satisfaction.

Our Capabilities

FOLSME International Limited excels in a wide spectrum of operations spanning Mining, Engineering, Generators & Power Solutions, Logistics, Construction, and Import/Export Services. Our expertise integrates technology, skilled manpower, and innovative approaches to deliver high-quality solutions that meet and exceed client expectations. We manage complex projects with precision, ensuring efficiency, sustainability, and safety at every stage of execution. From operational planning to end-to-end project delivery, FOLSME is your trusted partner for transformative results in the industries we serve.

In Mining & Minerals, we specialize in mineral exploration, extraction, and processing of key resources like barite, gypsum, kaolin, zinc, coal, gold, and iron ore. Our mining services in Nigeria leverage advanced techniques to ensure environmentally responsible operations, complying with local regulations and international standards for sustainable mining practices.

Our Energy & Engineering division provides comprehensive solutions including power generation, electrical engineering, and mechanical services. We offer reliable generators and power solutions tailored for industrial, commercial, and residential needs, ensuring uninterrupted energy supply across Nigeria and beyond.

For Construction, FOLSME delivers turnkey projects from infrastructure development to building construction. Our team of certified engineers and skilled workers executes projects with a focus on quality, timeliness, and cost-effectiveness, contributing to Nigeria's growing infrastructure landscape.

In Logistics, we provide end-to-end supply chain management, transportation, and warehousing solutions. Our logistics services in Nigeria ensure seamless movement of goods, with expertise in road, rail, and multimodal transport to optimize delivery times and reduce costs.

Our Oil & Gas capabilities include exploration support, pipeline construction, and maintenance services. We adhere to stringent safety protocols and environmental guidelines to support Nigeria's energy sector with reliable and efficient solutions.

Through Trade & Commerce, FOLSME facilitates import and export of minerals, machinery, and industrial equipment. Our import export services connect Nigerian businesses with global markets, ensuring compliance with customs regulations and competitive pricing.

With over 15 years of experience, FOLSME International has built a reputation for excellence in Nigeria's industrial sectors. Our commitment to innovation, safety, and sustainability drives us to exceed expectations and deliver lasting value to our clients. Contact us today to discover how our comprehensive capabilities can support your next project.
