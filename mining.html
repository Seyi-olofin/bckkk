Facts & Highlights

  • Nigeria holds over 44 mineral types in commercial quantities, across more than 500 locations.
  • Major deposits include iron ore, gold, coal, barite, gypsum, kaolin, limestone, bitumen, and lead/zinc.
  • Itakpe in Kogi State is home to a large iron ore reserve (estimated 3 billion tonnes).
  • Barite reserves exceed 7.5 million tonnes found in Taraba and Bauchi states.
  • Bentonite and baryte are essential in drilling fluids for oil wells; Nigeria produces both minerals.
  • Bitumen reserves are estimated at ~42 billion tonnes—among the world’s largest known.
